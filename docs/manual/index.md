The manual is for anyone who runs wayhouse: installing it, adding routes and nodes,
publishing sites, handling certificates, upgrading a fleet. Each page answers one task and
shows the config snippet, where to click in the UI, and what to check afterwards.

> [!NOTE]
> The manual is being written. Until a task has its own page, the guides below are the
> closest match, and the [Deep dive](../deep-dive/index.md) explains how each feature works.
